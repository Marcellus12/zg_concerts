📅 How to Subscribe (Recommended)

Subscribe to this URL to see new concerts appear automatically in your calendar app.

Google Calendar (Android/Desktop):
1. Open Google Calendar.
2. Click the + next to "Other calendars" (left sidebar).
3. Select "From URL".
4. Paste this link:
https://marcellus12.github.io/zg_concerts/zagreb_gigs.ics
iPhone / Apple Calendar:
1. Open Settings > Calendar.
2. Tap Accounts > Add Account > Other.
3. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar.
4. Paste the URL above.
